Privacy

Privacy policy

Last updated July 2026.

What's stored, and where

Your current wheel options, sound preference, theme choice, and recent spin history are saved with your browser's local storage, on your device only. Nothing is sent to a server, because this tool doesn't have one — it runs entirely client-side.

Shareable links

The "copy share link" button encodes your options directly into the URL. Anyone you send that link to will see those options — don't include anything you wouldn't want shared.

Cookies and analytics

This site uses Google Analytics to understand which pages are popular and how the site is used in aggregate. Google Analytics sets cookies and collects information such as your approximate location, device and browser type, and the pages you visit. This data is processed by Google under its own privacy policy. You can opt out using a browser extension like Google's Analytics Opt-out Add-on, or by using your browser's tracking-protection or cookie-blocking settings.

Third-party resources

Fonts and sound effects are loaded from third-party CDNs (Google Fonts and Freesound), and analytics are provided by Google Analytics. Those services may log basic request data such as IP address, under their own privacy policies.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the contact details listed on the About page.
