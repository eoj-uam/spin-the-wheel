How to play

Wedge Dodge

Left / right arrows (or A / D). Dodge the falling wedges — it speeds up fast.

How to play

  • Left / right arrows or A / D to move the cart
  • On touch devices: drag on the board or use the on-screen buttons
  • Wedges fall faster and thicker over time
  • Score is how long you survive, in seconds
  • One hit ends the run

Whack the Prize

Click the prize before it disappears. 30 seconds, and it only gets faster.

How to play

  • Click the prize circle as soon as it appears
  • Each hit shrinks the window a little more
  • 30 seconds on the clock, no penalty for a miss
  • Chase your best score before time runs out

Ticket Muncher

Arrow keys or WASD. Eat every ticket — grab a big golden ticket and the inspectors turn edible for a few seconds.

How to play

  • Arrow keys or WASD to move through the maze
  • On touch devices: swipe on the board or use the on-screen arrows
  • Eat every ticket to win — the inspectors get faster as the maze empties
  • The four big golden tickets turn the inspectors blue and edible for a few seconds — eat them for bonus points
  • Getting caught costs a life; three lives and you're out

Balloon Rise

Tap, click, or press space to rise. Thread the gaps between the poles — don't clip one.

How to play

  • Tap or click anywhere on the board to rise
  • Spacebar works too, if you're on a keyboard
  • Gravity pulls you down between taps
  • It speeds up the longer you last
