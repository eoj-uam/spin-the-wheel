Free Prize Wheel for Giveaways, Raffles, and Social Media Contests

Picking a giveaway winner badly is a fast way to lose trust with the exact audience you were trying to reward. "I just picked someone" doesn't hold up when people have entered, shared, tagged friends, or paid for a raffle ticket — they want to see the pick happen, and they want it to obviously be random. A spin wheel solves both problems at once: it's visual, it's public, and nobody can quietly thumb the scale.

Why a spin wheel beats a "comment picker" bot

Comment-picker tools are common for social media giveaways, but they have a trust problem: the selection happens behind the scenes, in a screenshot you're asked to take on faith. A wheel spun live — on a stream, in a video, or on a screen share — lets your audience watch the actual random moment happen. That's the difference between "trust me" and "watch this."

How to run a fair, provable spin

  • Load every eligible entry before you start recording or going live. Adding names after people have seen the wheel invites "you added them last minute" complaints, even if it's not true.
  • Record or screen-share the actual spin — not just the result. A screenshot of a name on a wheel proves nothing; a video of it landing does.
  • Say the rules out loud before you spin. "Everyone who entered by 5pm and followed the account" — stated before the wheel moves, not adjusted after.
  • Post the winner and the clip together when announcing, so anyone can verify it themselves rather than taking your word for it.

Weighting entries for raffles with multiple tickets

Most giveaways aren't strictly one-entry-per-person — someone who bought three raffle tickets, or referred two friends, should have better odds than someone with a single entry. This wheel supports that directly: type a name followed by x2, x3, or however many entries they've earned (for example Alex x3, Priya, Jordan x2), and the wheel gives each person exactly that many times the chance — visibly, as extra wedges, not as a hidden multiplier. The full mechanics are in the weighted wheel guide if you want to see the math.

Streaming and social media tips

  • Use the Neon theme (top right of the wheel) if you're streaming — it reads better on camera and on video than the default light theme.
  • The copy share link button encodes your whole entry list into a URL, so you can hand it to a co-host or moderator to run the spin without you retyping every name.
  • Save as image after the spin to post a clean screenshot of the final wheel alongside your announcement.

Before you go live: a quick checklist

  • Entry list finalized and double-checked for duplicate or misspelled names
  • Weighting applied correctly if some entries earned extra chances
  • Rules stated clearly, before the spin, not adjusted after
  • Recording or screen share running before you hit spin, not started after

Load your entry list and run the spin live — it's free, no account needed, and works the same whether you're picking one winner or fifty.
