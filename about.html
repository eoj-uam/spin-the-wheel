About

A wheel for when nobody can agree

Spin the Wheel Decision Maker started as a small tool for one very specific argument: where to eat. It's grown into a general-purpose picker people use for game nights, classroom raffles, chore rotations, and anything else that benefits from a fair, random call.

What it does

Type in your options, hit spin, and the wheel picks one at random. No accounts, no ads blocking the wheel, no tracking beyond what's needed to remember your last set of choices on your own device.

Why it's fair

Every spin uses your browser's random number generator to choose a wedge, then animates the wheel to land on it. Options with an x2 or x3 suffix simply get extra wedges, so their odds scale exactly the way you'd expect.

Who builds it

This site is maintained independently and updated based on how people actually use it. If something's confusing or broken, that's useful to know — see the blog for updates and the privacy page for how your data is handled.
